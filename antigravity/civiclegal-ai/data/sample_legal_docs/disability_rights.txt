AMERICANS WITH DISABILITIES ACT (ADA) — 42 U.S.C. § 12101
=============================================================

The ADA prohibits discrimination against people with disabilities in:
- Employment (Title I)
- State and local government services (Title II)
- Public accommodations (Title III)
- Telecommunications (Title IV)

TITLE I — EMPLOYMENT (Employers with 15+ employees)
Covered employers must:
- Not discriminate in hiring, firing, pay, promotions, training
- Provide reasonable accommodations unless it causes undue hardship
- Keep medical information confidential

Definition of Disability (42 U.S.C. § 12102):
1. A physical or mental impairment that substantially limits a major life activity
2. A record of such impairment
3. Being regarded as having such impairment

How to Request a Workplace Accommodation:
1. Notify your employer in writing
2. Describe the limitation caused by your disability
3. Request specific accommodation (flexible schedule, modified equipment, etc.)
4. Engage in interactive process with employer

The employer may request documentation from a healthcare provider.

TITLE II — STATE AND LOCAL GOVERNMENT
- Government programs must be accessible
- Physical access required (ramps, elevators, accessible parking)
- Effective communication (sign language interpreters, documents in accessible formats)

TITLE III — PUBLIC ACCOMMODATIONS (Restaurants, Hotels, Stores)
- Must remove architectural barriers where readily achievable
- Service animals must be permitted
- Cannot charge extra fees for accessibility services

FILING A COMPLAINT:
- Employment discrimination: EEOC — https://www.eeoc.gov/filing-charge-discrimination
- Public access / gov services: DOJ — https://www.ada.gov/filing_complaint.htm
- Deadline: 180 days from discriminatory act (300 days in some states)
