What CivicLegal AI Does

🔍

Legal Rights Navigator

Ask plain-language questions about your legal rights — tenant rights, disability accommodations, constitutional protections, immigration basics, and more. Get cited, grounded answers.

💰

Benefits Finder

Enter your household profile and get automatically matched to federal benefit programs: SNAP, Medicaid, CHIP, WIC, TANF, Section 8, SSI, SSDI, EITC, and LIHEAP.

📄

Document Upload

Upload legal documents (PDF, DOCX, TXT) for contextual analysis. The RAG pipeline indexes your document for informed question answering.

🛡️

Anti-Hallucination Guard

5-layer safety system ensures responses are grounded in retrieved sources, not invented. Minimum 50% cosine similarity required. Every response includes a mandatory disclaimer.

📚

Citation Cards

Every answer includes clickable citation cards showing the source, relevance score, and direct link to authoritative legal sources like CourtListener and Congress.gov.

🎯

Confidence Scoring

Color-coded confidence badges (High/Medium/Low) tell you how well-grounded each answer is, helping you make informed decisions about seeking further legal counsel.

🛡️ Anti-Hallucination System

1
Constrained Prompt

System prompt explicitly restricts to retrieved legal information only.

2
Temperature = 0

Deterministic generation — no creative hallucination.

3
Grounding Check

Cosine similarity ≥ 0.50 required. Below threshold → safe refusal.

4
Overconfidence Detection

Flags phrases like "you will definitely win" — caps confidence at 70%.

5
Mandatory Disclaimer

Legal disclaimer injected on every single response. No exceptions.
